# Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026. Applies to Tesla Overlay for Windows and macOS.

**Tesla Overlay does not collect, store or share any personal data.**

Your video clips are processed entirely on your own computer. They are never uploaded anywhere, and the app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

The only internet access the app makes is downloading map images (tiles) from OpenStreetMap when you use a map feature (the corner map, the route image or the map video). Like any map app, those requests tell OpenStreetMap's servers which area is being shown, and include your device's IP address. Map images are saved on your computer so the same area isn't downloaded again. OpenStreetMap's own privacy policy is at https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy. If you don't use the map features, the app makes no network requests at all.

The app only opens the clips you choose, and saves the videos and maps it makes next to them or in a folder you pick.

GPS coordinates from your clips are only drawn on a video if you turn on the GPS coordinates option, which is off by default. Keep in mind that maps and coordinates in videos you share can show where a drive took place.

Settings such as units and theme are stored on your computer only.

Questions: open an issue on this project's GitHub page.
