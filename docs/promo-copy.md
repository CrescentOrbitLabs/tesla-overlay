## Short blurb

Tesla Overlay is a free Windows and Mac app that reads the frame-by-frame driving data Tesla hides inside its dashcam clips (speed, gear, steering, pedals, turn signals, FSD state and GPS) and makes a new copy of your video with it on screen: a speed panel, a blue FSD-style path on the road, and a live map in the corner that turns with the car. It can also export a map of the whole drive. Everything runs on your computer; nothing is uploaded.
