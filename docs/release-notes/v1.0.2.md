Tesla Overlay 1.0.2. Turn Tesla dashcam clips into videos with your speed, an FSD-style driving path and a live map, burned right in.

![Tesla Overlay](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/joshuah-1998/tesla-overlay/main/docs/images/before-after.png)

### What's new

- A new Hardware menu with Auto, HW3 and HW4. Auto works out which hardware recorded the clip from its video size: HW3 records every camera at 1280 × 960, while HW4 records the front camera at 2896 × 1876 and the other cameras at 1448 × 938. If a clip was resized and gets detected wrong, pick HW3 or HW4 yourself.
- The hardware sets the camera's field of view used to draw the driving path, so the path lines up with the road on both HW3 and HW4 cars.
- The hardware version now appears next to the date, time and heading in the video. Turn off Show hardware in video to hide it.
- Command line: `--hw=auto|hw3|hw4` and `--no-hw-label`.

The apps aren't signed with paid developer certificates yet. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, open the app once, then click Open Anyway under System Settings > Privacy & Security. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of every file.

### Which clips work

Dashcam clips (SavedClips and RecentClips, not Sentry) recorded on firmware 2025.44.25 or later, on HW3 or newer.
