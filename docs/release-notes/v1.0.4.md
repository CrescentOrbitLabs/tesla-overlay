Tesla Overlay 1.0.4. Turn Tesla dashcam clips into videos with your speed, an FSD-style driving path and a live map, burned right in.

![Tesla Overlay](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/joshuah-1998/tesla-overlay/main/docs/images/before-after.png)

### What's new

- **Crescent Orbit Labs.** Tesla Overlay is now published by Crescent Orbit Labs LLC, and a new splash screen plays when the app opens. The first launch of each version shows the full animation, later launches a short one. Click or press any key to skip it; it's skipped automatically when your system's reduce motion setting is on.
- **Email support.** Questions and problems can go to support@crescentorbitlabs.com, also reachable from the Help menu and the License panel.
- The Mac app has a new identifier, so macOS may ask you to confirm opening it one more time.

### Downloads

| Platform | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 and 11 | `TeslaOverlay-1.0.4-windows.zip` |
| macOS 11 or newer | `TeslaOverlay-1.0.4-mac.zip` |

The apps aren't signed with paid developer certificates yet. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, open the app once, then click Open Anyway under System Settings > Privacy & Security. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of every file.

### Which clips work

Dashcam clips (SavedClips and RecentClips, not Sentry) recorded on firmware 2025.44.25 or later, on HW3 or newer.
