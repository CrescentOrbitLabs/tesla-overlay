Tesla Overlay 1.0.1. Turn Tesla dashcam clips into videos with your speed, an FSD-style driving path and a live map, burned right in.

![Tesla Overlay](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/joshuah-1998/tesla-overlay/main/docs/images/before-after.png)

### What's new

- Tesla Overlay is now licensed under the PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0 instead of the MIT License. It's still free for personal and noncommercial use, but modifying or redistributing it is no longer permitted.
- The License panel's third-party notices tab is now labeled Third party.

There are no changes to how videos are made, so there's no need to reprocess any clips.

### Downloads

| Platform | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 and 11 | `TeslaOverlay-1.0.1-windows.zip` |
| macOS 11 or newer | `TeslaOverlay-1.0.1-mac.zip` |

The apps aren't signed with paid developer certificates yet. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, open the app once, then click Open Anyway under System Settings > Privacy & Security. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of every file.

### Which clips work

Dashcam clips (SavedClips and RecentClips, not Sentry) recorded on firmware 2025.44.25 or later, on HW3 or newer.
