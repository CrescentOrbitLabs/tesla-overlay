Tesla Overlay 1.0.3. Turn Tesla dashcam clips into videos with your speed, an FSD-style driving path and a live map, burned right in.

![Tesla Overlay](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/joshuah-1998/tesla-overlay/main/docs/images/before-after.png)

### What's new

- A new Advanced menu in the Overlay card for details Tesla's clips don't record:
  - Speed profile: Sloth, Chill, Standard, Hurry or Mad Max.
  - Car model: every Tesla model and trim, from Model 3 to Cybertruck Cyberbeast.
  - FSD version: every release from v9 to v14.3, including the HW3 Lite builds, or type an exact version such as 14.3.13.
- Anything you set is shown on the video in a second strip next to the date and time. Clear all removes it.
- Command line: `--model`, `--fsd` and `--profile`.

### Downloads

| Platform | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 and 11 | `TeslaOverlay-1.0.3-windows.zip` |
| macOS 11 or newer | `TeslaOverlay-1.0.3-mac.zip` |

The apps aren't signed with paid developer certificates yet. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, open the app once, then click Open Anyway under System Settings > Privacy & Security. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of every file.

### Which clips work

Dashcam clips (SavedClips and RecentClips, not Sentry) recorded on firmware 2025.44.25 or later, on HW3 or newer.
