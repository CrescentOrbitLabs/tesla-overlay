Tesla Overlay 1.0.0, the first public release. Turn Tesla dashcam clips into videos with your speed, an FSD-style driving path and a live map, burned right in.

![Tesla Overlay](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OWNER/REPO/v1.0.0/docs/images/before-after.png)

### Highlights

- Speed panel with speed, gear, steering, turn signals, FSD / Autosteer / TACC badge, date, time and heading
- Pedal bars that show what the car is doing while FSD drives
- FSD-style driving path on the road, built from the car's recorded heading
- Live heading-up mini map in the corner of the video
- Route map image and animated map video exports (OpenStreetMap)
- Liquid glass interface with light and dark modes, live preview and batch processing
- Windows 10 and 11, and macOS 11 or newer on Apple Silicon and Intel

### Downloads

| Platform | File |
| --- | --- |

The apps aren't signed with paid developer certificates yet. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, open the app once, then click Open Anyway under System Settings > Privacy & Security. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of every file.

### Which clips work

Dashcam clips (SavedClips and RecentClips, not Sentry) recorded on firmware 2025.44.25 or later, on HW3 or newer.
